# SCHEMA.md — Engineering Diplomacy case schema (v2)

Fill this **completely** for every candidate that clears the Stage-1 gate, **before**
writing a word of prose. If a required field cannot be filled crisply and truthfully, the
case is not a fit — drop it.

This is the formalization gate. Its value is exactly what the Nature paper describes:
turning an informal argument into a form that must be made fully explicit reconstructs
omitted steps and exposes tacit assumptions. Tao found a real gap in his own published
proof this way. Expect the schema to kill cases that felt good in prose.

The schema is thick; the prose stays lean. Most of these fields never appear as labeled
sections in the writeup — they discipline it.

---

## Identity

- **case_id** — `kebab-case-actor-event`
- **title** — the case study's title
- **case_type** — `documented` | `composite` *(composite = illustrative teaching case;
  individual scale only; must be labeled; no invented sources)*
- **mode** — `retrospective` | `prospective`
- **scale** — `individual` | `community` | `international` *(enforced by quota)*
- **region**, **recency** (`recent` = 2010+ / `historical`), **year**
- **rubric_version** — the `QUALITY_RUBRIC.md` version this case was written under

## The three perspectives — ALL THREE REQUIRED

Every case is examined from all three seats before it is written, whichever one ends up
dominating the prose. The workshop's Session-2 panel is the same triad under different
names (Scientific / Social / Institutional).

- **scientist** — *What do we know?* The facts, the model, the measurable.
- **humanist** — *Who is affected, and what is contested?* Meaning, trust, identity, fear.
- **engineer** — *What is buildable, and who can act, coordinate, or block?* Constraints,
  standing, feasibility.
- **archetype_dominant** — `scientist` | `humanist` | `engineer` | `mixed`
- **crowded_out_mode** — which of thinking / feeling / doing was displaced, and by which

## Diagnosis of the problem space

- **scientific_facts** — what is known, and how well
- **social_facts** — what is contested, by whom, and why it matters to them
- **political_realities** — who can act, who can block, what is politically survivable
- **uncertainty_and_ambiguity** — what is genuinely unknown (uncertainty) vs. what means
  different things to different parties (ambiguity). Do not collapse these.
- **key_tensions** — 2–3, stated as trade-offs *(Equity vs. Reliability; Science vs.
  Politics; Short-term vs. Long-term)*
- **problem_definition** — one sentence. The decision problem, not the topic.

## The metaphors

- **one_plus_one** — what question was each party actually answering? Where did shared
  understanding fail to exist, such that 1+1 produced two separate ones rather than 2?
- **where_is_x** — the precise intervention point, and why it is the fulcrum. The most
  leveraged intervention is rarely the largest; it is the most precisely located.
- **eighteenth_camel** — the option outside the stated binary. Must be a *third thing*, not
  a compromise between the two. If none existed, say so and defend it — that is a finding.
- **solomon_check** — was the required wisdom available but not directed inward? Would these
  actors have diagnosed this correctly in someone else's system?
- **urgency_vs_actionability** — how did urgency outrun the capacity to act meaningfully?

## Conditions, capacity, constraints

- **enabling_conditions** — what made a better outcome possible (emerging, weakening, missing)
- **capacities** — what the actors could actually do
- **constraints** — what bounded them; which was **binding**

## Decision pathway (the workshop spine)

- **decision_pathway** — exactly one: `Implement` | `Redesign` | `Expand` | `Reframe`.
  Who acts, for whom, through what process. For retrospective cases this is
  counterfactual — the move that was available at the decisive moment.
- **tools** — 2–4, each as `tool → purpose → how it changes interaction, learning, or
  coordination among stakeholders`. A tool that doesn't change one of those three is
  decoration.
- **metrics** — 2–4, each as `metric → measure → method`. Measurable and trackable, and
  visibly linked to the pathway.
- **ninety_day_commitment** — who does what next. Prospective: a live recommendation.
  Retrospective: what the first 90 days of the counterfactual pathway would have contained.

## Stress test and refinement

- **stress_test** — answer all five: What political event could derail this? What
  institution could block it? What assumption may be wrong? What resource is missing? What
  would make this fail within 12 months?
- **revised_problem_definition** — restate `problem_definition` in light of the stress
  test. **If it comes back unchanged, the stress test was too soft. Go again.**

## Honesty

- **flag** — the limitation the episode reveals that principled pragmatism does *not*
  resolve. Prefer a fresh one over re-surfacing ego or organizational power.
- **sources** — 3–6 with verification status (`documented` cases only; `composite` cases
  carry `sources: none — illustrative composite`).

---

## Drop criteria

Drop the candidate if you cannot fill, crisply and truthfully, **any** of:
`three perspectives (all three)`, `where_is_x`, `eighteenth_camel`, `decision_pathway`,
`flag`. A tidy story with no flag, or a vague "they should have known better" pathway,
means the episode does not actually instantiate the pattern.
