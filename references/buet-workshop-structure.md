# Dhaka / BUET Engineering Diplomacy Workshop — Structure

*Reference: the decision spine the case pipeline is modeled on. Draft 06/25/26, Water Diplomacy (Ganges Treaty renewal) as the worked example. The agent uses this for the decision-pathway → tools → metrics sequence and the 7-component synthesis block.*

## Core logic

**Diagnosis of the Problem Space → Understanding of Conditions, Capacity, and Constraints → Decision Pathway → Tools → Metrics → Implementation → Learning by Doing.**

Actions are not assumed to cause desirable outcomes. The question is how existing conditions, capacity, and constraints make desirable outcomes possible *under uncertainty and ambiguity*.

Three thematic areas: Water, Energy, Cyber Security. Decision-focused and action-oriented, not discussion-focused.

## Anchor problem (worked example)

Renewal of the Ganges Water Treaty under uncertainty, variability, and political constraints.

**Guiding question:** What practical negotiation pathway can Bangladesh pursue to renew the Ganges Water Treaty in a way that improves dry-season reliability, protects equity, and remains politically feasible under climate uncertainty?

## Session 1 — Diagnosis

Why is this problem important, and what is at stake across stakeholder groups and scales? What are the likely consequences if it is addressed well or poorly? The expert presents **scientific facts + social facts + political realities + uncertainty and ambiguity.**
*Output: a problem of national significance.*

## Session 2 — Three perspectives (the panel)

- **Scientific:** what do we know?
- **Social:** who is affected and what is contested?
- **Institutional:** who can act, coordinate, or block?

*Output: multi-perspective insight into the identified problem.*

Worked example — scientific facts: dry-season flows are highly variable with declining trends; the existing treaty is fixed-allocation with limited flexibility; upstream abstraction affects downstream availability. Socio-political realities: trust deficit between India and Bangladesh; domestic political pressure in both; competing sectoral demands (agriculture, environment, urban).

**Key tensions:** Equity vs. Reliability · Science vs. Politics · Short-term vs. Long-term.

*Structured Q&A: surface the (mis)alignment among the three perspectives. Output: a synthesis focused on actionability.*

## Session 3 — Map the system; conditions, capacity, constraints

- System type: simple / complicated / complex
- Boundary mismatch: scale, domain, institutional
- What is contested: access, allocation, use, risk
- What is scientifically known, what is societally contested, what is economically and politically feasible
- Enabling conditions; capacities; constraints. Which is **binding**? What is the primary misalignment, tension, or blind spot that explains the problem's persistence? Identify emerging, weakening, and missing conditions.

## Session 4 — Decision pathway, tools, metrics

**Decision pathway.** Given the problem and the contextual capacity and constraints, what is actionable *now*? Who needs to act, for whom, through what process? Frame the pathway as **one clear move: Implement, Redesign, Expand, or Reframe.**

**Tools.** Which tools support and assess the chosen pathway (joint fact-finding, scenario modeling, mutual gains negotiation, adaptive treaty clauses, stakeholder mapping, mediation)? Each tool must explain **how it changes interaction, learning, or coordination** among stakeholders.
*Output: a tool–decision matrix.*

**Metrics.** What measurable, trackable success metrics assess outcomes, and how do they link to the pathway?
*Output: metric → measure → method.*

**Adaptive learning.** Name the key uncertainties and the trigger→response rules. (Extreme drought → renegotiate allocations. Data disagreement → activate joint verification. Policy shift → trigger review clause.)

## Final synthesis — stress test, then report

**Stress test the pathway:**
- What political event could derail this?
- What institution could block this?
- What assumption may be wrong?
- What resource is missing?
- What would make this pathway fail within 12 months?

**Report in exactly this format:**
1. **Problem** — what is the decision problem?
2. **Stakeholders** — who matters and why?
3. **Binding constraint** — what prevents action?
4. **Decision pathway** — Implement, redesign, expand, or reframe?
5. **Tools** — which will be used, and why?
6. **Metrics** — how will progress be tracked?
7. **90-day commitment** — who does what next?
