## Notes for human review

Both cases are at the upper range of prose quality for this corpus. The key judgment calls for human review:

1. **Criterion 1 (tri-perspective)** — Both cases score 5. Human reviewers should check whether the three readings feel genuinely independent or whether they converge too quickly on the same conclusion. In the Rana Plaza case, all three perspectives arrive at "the split is the structural problem"; the question is whether each really sees something the others miss.

2. **Criterion 9 (flag freshness)** — Both flags are new to the corpus. The Citicorp flag (ethics of voluntary quiet fix depend on repair-feasibility) and the Rana Plaza flag (standing as precondition for engineering diplomacy tools) are structurally different from all existing flags. Human reviewers may want to confirm neither overlaps with the Aral Sea flag ("principled pragmatism has no position when the diagnostic role itself has no political standing") — I believe Rana Plaza is about the preconditions for the tools themselves, while Aral Sea is about the diagnostic actor's political standing, but they are adjacent.

3. **Criterion 10 (prose)** — The Citicorp cold open opens with a descriptor rather than a pure scene. If this loses a point in human review, it would drop to 3 (since the rubric 5 requires "specific person, specific moment, date and place" without the described distance a summarizing phrase creates).
