## Notes for human reviewers

1. **Rubric arithmetic:** The maximum weighted total under the current rubric weights (24 total weight points × 5 max score = 120) does not match the rubric's stated "out of 110." This is flagged here for Prof. Islam's attention — the denominator may need correction in a future rubric version.

2. **Both cases are community scale.** This week's quota slot (community, the least-covered scale) and the open slot both landed at community scale. The open slot is allowed any scale; Grenfell was the highest-scoring candidate on the Stage-2 fertility rubric (64/120 fertility score) partly because Europe (0 cases) is the largest regional gap. This produced two community cases in one week, which reduces variety. Arman or Prof. Islam may prefer to swap the open slot for an individual or international case in a future week.

3. **Flag adjacency:** The Grenfell flag ("constituted as an expert in their own risk environment — answered by power before principled analysis") is adjacent to the Challenger flag ("raw organizational power structure"). Both involve power suppressing correct technical input. The mechanisms differ (formal exclusion of tenants vs. hierarchical override of an engineer in a teleconference), but reviewers should assess whether this is substantively fresh enough for a 4 rather than a 3 on criterion 9.
